# Commercial & Government Production Licensing — WokeNet

**Transparency is free. Production is not.**

| Track | Rights | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Section A** | Read, audit, test, benchmark, non-commercial evaluation, security research | Free |
| **Section B** | Production, SaaS, commercial redistribution, government operational use | **Paid written license** |

Full legal text: [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)

---

## Why this model

| Goal | How |
| --- | --- |
| Public validation & trust | Source is visible; tests and benchmarks can be reproduced |
| Stop free SaaS clones | Production / commercial use needs Section B |
| Government must pay to operationalize | Explicit Section A(d) / Section B |
| Contributors welcome | CLA assigns copyright so dual-license stays enforceable |

This is **source-available dual licensing**, not OSI open source.  
OSI open source cannot require payment for ordinary production use. That is intentional.

---

## Who needs Section B

- Companies running WokeNet in production or as SaaS
- App stores / paid clients embedding substantial first-party code
- Government, military, EM, or public agencies in operational deployment
- Contractors delivering this stack to public-sector customers
- Anyone selling a rebranded or white-label fork

## Who does not (Section A only)

- Auditors and security researchers
- Students and academic researchers (non-commercial)
- Individual developers evaluating locally
- Contributors opening PRs under the CLA

---

## How to request a license

1. Open an issue titled **Commercial / Government License Request**  
   https://github.com/AlexBTC420/wokenet/issues/new  
2. Or contact [github.com/AlexBTC420](https://github.com/AlexBTC420)

Include: legal entity, use case, expected scale (users/nodes/revenue band), territory, duration, modification needs, procurement constraints.

### Indicative tiers (not a binding offer)

| Tier | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Indie non-commercial personal | Section A |
| Startup production | Annual Section B |
| Enterprise SaaS | Revenue share and/or enterprise fee |
| Government / multi-agency | Enterprise Section B + compliance terms |
| Exclusive OEM | Premium; rare |

**No token payment for licenses. Standard commercial invoicing.**
