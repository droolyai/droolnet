01 · The wire

What we cover — and what we refuse.

Continuity Bureau organizes signal for humans everywhere: cities, labs, studios, coasts, deserts, stations. Crypto and infrastructure appear only when they are news with receipts — never as ideology.

We publish

Documented reality

Verified events, methods, measurements, culture that can be sourced, disasters and recoveries, science that can be checked.

We refuse

Party propaganda

No candidate endorsements. No “team red / team blue” framing. No rage farming dressed as journalism.

We disclose

Uncertainty

What we know, what we don’t, what is staged vs live. Corrections append. History stays readable.

03 · Standards

Organized media has rules.

  1. Apolitical by design. No endorsements. No campaign ops. Cover power with documents, not jerseys.
  2. Worldwide beat. Local stories matter; no region is “filler.” Web3 is one topic among many.
  3. Receipts travel with claims. Sources, methods, uncertainty — visible on the dispatch.
  4. Protect people. Consent and safety beat the scoop.
  5. Corrections append. We do not memory-hole mistakes.
  6. Honest staging. Research tools and live news are labeled separately. No fake “fully decentralized news chain” claims.
